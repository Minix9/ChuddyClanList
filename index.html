List re-rank

Every placement on the list has been reviewed using eight placement opinions from players who have played the levels. Opinions based on completions counted fully, opinions based on progress counted half, and each opinion's confidence rating set how much it mattered.

Summary

  • The new order matches 99% of all opinions, up from 96%.
  • The 7 levels nobody gave an opinion on kept their spot relative to their neighbours.
